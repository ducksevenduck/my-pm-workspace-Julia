## The Product

*What it does, for whom, current focus.*

Streakly is a consumer habit + micro-learning app (languages, guitar, coding, chess) where a daily streak is the core habit loop.

**My role:** PM owning the Engagement squad (home screen, daily lesson loop, streak mechanics, push notifications). Triad: Raj (Senior Engineer), Lena (Product Designer), me. I report to Marcus, Head of Product.

**Core metric:** Day-7 retention — dropped from 48% to 39% since the v2 streak/notification redesign.

**Key tension:** The streak mechanic that drives the habit loop is the same mechanic that punishes a lapse — breaking a streak resets to zero with no acknowledgment or path back. Roughly 80% of users who miss two days never return.

**Current focus / open decision:** Two workstreams — (1) fixing notification tone (drop the guilt-based framing, replace "you lost your streak" language with something more encouraging), and (2) designing an easy comeback experience triggered after a user misses X days (threshold still TBD). Candidate comeback features (best-streak stat, 60-second lesson, one-tap streak-freeze) are on the table but not finalized.

**Constraints (load every session):** In discovery — no designs, no committed scope, 8-week window from sprint kickoff. Solution should use existing data/instrumentation, not new infrastructure.
