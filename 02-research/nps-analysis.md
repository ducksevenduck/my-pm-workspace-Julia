# NPS Feedback Findings — Streak & Comeback Experience

*For: Marcus. Source: 10 raw NPS comments. Prepared during discovery for the Comeback Experience project.*

## Summary

10 comments were reviewed. The overwhelming majority (8 of 10) center on the same underlying pain point already driving the Day-7 retention drop: the streak resets to zero after a miss, with no graceful way back, and for several users that single moment ended their relationship with the app entirely.

## Themes Mentioned More Than Once (Ranked by Frequency)

| Rank | Theme | Mentions | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Streak resets to zero and punishes a single miss | 4 | "I hit a 20-day streak, missed one day, and it reset to zero... Felt pointless to start over." |
| 2 | Users want an active recovery mechanism (freeze, useful pull-back) | 3 | "Other apps let you freeze a streak. Why not this one?" |
| 3 | Losing the streak ends engagement entirely (total abandonment) | 3 | "The streak is the only thing keeping me engaged, but the second I lost it, I was done." |
| 4 | Notifications feel like nagging or come too frequently | 2 | "I got three in one afternoon and just turned them all off." |

*Note: one comment ("The home screen looks the same whether I'm on a 2-day streak or coming back after two weeks away") raised a related but single-mention point — the app doesn't acknowledge where a user currently is in their journey. Worth tracking even though it didn't repeat.*

## Praise vs. Complaints

**Praise (2 comments):**
- "The first week was genuinely fun."
- "Love the lessons."

**Complaints (8 comments):** all other comments — centered on the punitive reset, lack of a recovery path, notification frequency/tone, and the app's silence on where a returning user actually stands.

## Top 3 Problem Areas (by Frequency/Impact)

1. **No graceful path back after a broken streak.** This is the single largest and most consistent signal — 7 of 10 comments touch it directly or indirectly. Users describe the reset to zero as punishing and are explicitly asking why there's no way back in.
2. **The streak is carrying all the engagement weight, so losing it ends the relationship.** Several users said losing the streak made the app feel "pointless" or "meaningless" — for them, engagement doesn't survive a single lapse.
3. **Notification frequency and tone are pushing users to opt out.** At least one user disabled notifications entirely after receiving three in one afternoon — a signal that the current notification approach is actively working against re-engagement, not just failing to help it.

## How This Corroborates the Problem Statement

This feedback is independent evidence for the same problem already described in [project.md](../01-orient/project.md)'s Evidence Summary — it doesn't surface a new problem area, and no solutions are proposed here.
